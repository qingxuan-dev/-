质量规则（原文）Quality rules (from the original)

  1. 数字必须带年份和出处。Every figure carries a year and a source.
  2. 解释不等于正当化：历史条目只讲机制，不替任何一方辩护。Explaining is not justifying: historical entries describe mechanisms and defend no side.
  3. 有争议的政治问题，给出主要观点，不下结论。For contested political questions, give the main views and no verdict.
  4. 推理链里的每一步都要能单独检验。Every step of a reasoning chain must be independently checkable.
