写给所有人：量子力学的三个反直觉之处For everyone: three ways quantum mechanics defies intuition

1. 概率不能抵消，振幅可以（QM-14）1. Probabilities can’t cancel; amplitudes can (QM-14)

掷骰子时，两种走法的概率只能相加。量子力学先把两条路径的振幅相加，再取平方得到概率。振幅带有相位，相位相反时两条路会互相抵消：两条缝都开着，某些位置反而一个粒子也到不了。这就是双缝实验里的暗纹。

With dice, the probabilities of two routes can only add. Quantum mechanics adds the two paths’ amplitudes first and then squares to get a probability. Amplitudes carry a phase, and when the phases are opposite the two paths cancel: with both slits open, some places receive no particles at all. These are the dark fringes of the double-slit experiment.

2. 测量方向决定你看到什么（QM-17）2. The measurement direction decides what you see (QM-17)

电子的自旋只有“上”“下”两个测量结果，但它的状态可以指向任意方向。蓝色箭头是状态，灰色轴是你选择的测量方向。得到“+”的概率是 ½(1 + cos 夹角)。“上和下的等权叠加”沿 z 测是 50/50，沿 x 测却是 100%：它本身就是一个指向 x 的确定状态。

An electron’s spin gives only two measurement results, “up” and “down”, but its state can point in any direction. The blue arrow is the state and the grey axis is the direction you measure along. The probability of “+” is ½(1 + cos of the angle between them). An “equal superposition of up and down” gives 50/50 along z but 100% along x: it is itself a definite state pointing along x.

3. 结果不是事先写好的：贝尔不等式（QM-19）3. Results are not written in advance: Bell’s inequality (QM-19)

如果两个纠缠粒子的测量结果出发时就已经“写好”，那么两人测量结果的相关程度有一个上限：CHSH 组合 |S| 最多是 2。量子力学预言可以达到 2√2 ≈ 2.83，实验测到了超过 2 的值，2022 年诺贝尔物理学奖表彰了这些实验。下图是两个测量方向相差 Δ 时的相关系数：曲线是量子力学，折线是“事先写好答案”的模型能做到的最好结果。

If the results for two entangled particles were already “written” when they set off, the correlation between two observers’ results would have a ceiling: the CHSH combination |S| is at most 2. Quantum mechanics predicts up to 2√2 ≈ 2.83, experiments have measured values above 2, and the 2022 Nobel Prize in Physics honoured those experiments. The chart shows the correlation when the two measurement directions differ by Δ: the curve is quantum mechanics, the straight line is the best a “predetermined answers” model can do.

可信度标签：读每一条时先看它Credibility labels: check these first

已确立Established实验和理论都很牢靠，例如叠加、不确定性原理、贝尔不等式被违反。Solid in both experiment and theory, e.g. superposition, the uncertainty principle, the violation of Bell’s inequality.
前沿Frontier有理论或实验，但没有定论，例如物质为什么多于反物质、时间是否涌现。Theory or experiments exist but nothing is settled, e.g. why matter outnumbers antimatter, whether time is emergent.
推测Speculative目前没有可检验的证据，例如热时间假说、“宇宙在渲染”。No testable evidence so far, e.g. the thermal time hypothesis, “the universe is rendering”.

训练使用说明Using it for training

监督微调Supervised fine-tuning

输入用 question，输出依次拼接 reasoning_chain 与 answer。保留可信度标签，让模型学会在回答里区分“已确立”和“前沿”。

Use question as input and concatenate reasoning_chain and answer as output. Keep the credibility labels so the model learns to separate “established” from “frontier” in its answers.

偏好 / 负例Preference data / negatives

common_errors 与纠错记录给出了常见的错误说法。可以把同一问题下“含该错误的回答”作为被拒绝的一侧。

common_errors and the correction log list common wrong claims. An answer to the same question that contains such an error can serve as the rejected side.

可验证奖励Verifiable rewards

类型为“计算”的 4 条（QM-02、14、17、19）有确定的数值答案；另有 QM-05、15、16、20 也带验算。check 字段给出验算过程，适合自动判分。

The 4 “calculation” entries (QM-02, 14, 17, 19) have definite numerical answers, and QM-05, 15, 16 and 20 also include checks. The check field shows the working, suitable for automatic grading.
